## How each source should be used

- **Dallas City Council agendas and minutes:** An agenda proposal is not an approved award. Find the decision and named executing contractor. Verification: [source](https://dallascityhall.com/government/citysecretary/Pages/cMeetings.aspx).
- **Fort Worth procurement and bids:** Bid solicitations are discovery hints, not awards. Bonfire may require separate access. Verification: [source](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/finance/bids).
- **Fort Worth City Secretary public documents:** Check event dates and completion status; newly indexed records may describe old projects. Verification: [source](https://publicdocuments.fortworthtexas.gov/CSODOCS/DocView.aspx?dbid=0&id=360665&repo=City-Secretary).
- **TxDOT statewide letting and awards:** Distinguish letting schedules, bid tabs, apparent low bidders and final award decisions. Verification: [source](https://www.txdot.gov/business/road-bridge-maintenance/contract-letting.html).
- **SAM.gov contract opportunities:** API key and quotas apply. Most opportunity notices are not completed awards. Verification: [source](https://sam.gov/).
- **USAspending federal award data:** Transactions may be modifications or obligations on an existing award. Use award identity and action dates. Verification: [source](https://www.usaspending.gov/).
- **Construction Dive:** Corroborate company identity and event dates with primary sources. Verification: [source](https://www.constructiondive.com/).
- **Engineering News-Record:** Some articles require a subscription. Keep the reference and seek accessible primary corroboration. Verification: [source](https://www.enr.com/).
- **Dallas Innovates:** Editorial and sponsored/company-supplied material must retain their different provenance. Verification: [source](https://dallasinnovates.com/).
- **Dallas Business Journal:** Subscription-dependent coverage; no paywall bypass. Verification: [source](https://www.bizjournals.com/dallas/).
- **Business Wire:** Syndicated copies of one announcement are one source lineage, not independent corroboration. Verification: [source](https://www.businesswire.com/).
- **Dallas Regional Chamber:** Identify the contractor/employer; the company opening a building may not employ the construction crew. Verification: [source](https://www.dallaschamber.org/).
- **Fort Worth Economic Development Partnership:** Planned investment does not establish an award, mobilization or apparel requirement. Verification: [source](https://fortworthedp.com/).
- **KPost Company official website:** Company self-report; verify each event, its timing and apparel relevance separately. Verification: [source](https://kpostcompany.com/).
- **BakerTriangle official website:** Company self-report; verify each event, its timing and apparel relevance separately. Verification: [source](https://bakertriangle.com/).

## Search coverage

All six companies were included in four domain-filtered searches: LinkedIn, Instagram, X/Twitter and Indeed (24 submitted queries). The web index returned useful LinkedIn and Indeed matches; it returned no relevant Instagram or X results in these batches, despite official websites linking to several accounts. This is a real coverage gap, not evidence of inactivity. Facebook, YouTube and TikTok accounts were inventoried where linked; their feeds were not searched in this pass.

Live background monitoring is not running. The reusable search runner defaults to a preview and requires a Tavily API key for execution. All returned items remain research candidates until source, date, company, geography and commercial relevance are checked.
